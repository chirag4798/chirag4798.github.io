A CDN that can
think in images.

Architected and led an AI-powered image delivery platform for Ajio, JioMart, and Tira, bringing real-time transformation into the shopping experience.

Explore the system ＋

The pipeline Automated product tagging, Stable Diffusion background generation, U2Net foreground extraction, and SRGAN upscaling run as coordinated image services.

The engineering Python and FastAPI APIs orchestrate processing and platform integrations. I applied post-training quantization to lower latency and GPU memory use, and added token-usage tracking and efficiency improvements to reduce operating costs.

The outcome The platform contributed to a 5% increase in product engagement. I also owned production reliability work, including monitoring, incident response, and database recovery.

Video analytics,
